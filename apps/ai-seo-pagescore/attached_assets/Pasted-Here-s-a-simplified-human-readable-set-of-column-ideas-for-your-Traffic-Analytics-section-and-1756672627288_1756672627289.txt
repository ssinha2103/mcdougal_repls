Here’s a simplified, **human-readable set of column ideas** for your `Traffic Analytics` section and a **few additional sections** you can add. I’ve also included **only the most practical and relevant metrics** that are easy to extract from APIs like DataForSEO or similar sources.

---

## ✅ **Final Traffic Analytics Columns (5–6 Only)**

| Column Name          | Meaning                                             | How to Get It                                                  |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Organic Traffic**  | Monthly visits from Google organic search           | `etv` from `metrics.organic.etv` (DataForSEO)                  |
| **Ad Value**         | Cost to buy same traffic via ads (aka Traffic Cost) | `estimated_paid_traffic_cost`                                  |
| **Avg CPC**          | Average cost per click (if paid ads were run)       | `estimated_paid_traffic_cost ÷ etv`                            |
| **Top-10 %**         | % of keywords in Google top 10 results              | Count of keywords where `position <= 10` ÷ Total keyword count |
| **Visibility Score** | Overall visibility across all keywords              | Can be custom or derived from keyword rank distribution        |
| **Branded %**        | % of traffic coming from branded keywords           | From keyword filtering; not always available in DataForSEO     |

---

## ➕ **Additional Section Suggestions**

### 🔍 **Ranking Snapshot**

| Column Name    | What It Shows                         |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| **Top-3 KWs**  | Keywords ranking #1 to #3             |
| **Top-10 KWs** | Keywords in Top 10                    |
| **Pos 11–20**  | Keywords ranking on page 2            |
| **KWs Gained** | Keywords newly entered search results |
| **KWs Lost**   | Keywords no longer ranking            |

**🟩 Source**: DataForSEO `ranked_keywords` endpoint — group by `position` ranges.

---

### 🌱 **Growth Snapshot**

| Column Name      | What It Shows                        |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| **Traffic Δ**    | Change in ETV compared to last month |
| **Value Δ**      | Change in Traffic Cost (Ad Value)    |
| **KW Δ**         | Net keyword gain/loss                |
| **Top KWs Δ**    | Gain/loss of top 10 rankings         |
| **New Keywords** | Keywords ranked for the first time   |

You can calculate these by saving monthly snapshots and comparing.
