Methodology

Data Collection

Hospital data was compiled from multiple sources including:

  • Individual hospital websites and published service directories
  • Ohio Department of Health facility registries
  • Direct verification with hospital systems
  • News reports and public announcements for service closures

All data is regularly verified and updated. The "Last Verified" date shown for each hospital indicates when we last confirmed the information.

Service Definitions

Labor & Delivery (L&D)

Indicates whether a hospital provides labor and delivery services for childbirth. Hospitals marked "Yes" have active obstetric units.

NICU Levels

  • None: No neonatal intensive care unit
  • Level II: Special care nursery for moderately ill newborns
  • Level III: Intensive care for very ill or premature infants, with subspecialty services
  • Level IV: Highest level of care, including surgical capabilities and complex procedures

Distance & Travel Time Calculations

When you search by ZIP code, the map calculates:

  • Distance: Straight-line (haversine) distance in kilometers
  • Travel Time: If an OpenRouteService API key is configured, actual driving time via road network. Otherwise, estimated based on distance at 45 mph average speed.

Travel times are estimates and may vary based on traffic, weather, and road conditions. Always plan for extra time in emergency situations.

Closures Tracking

The closures timeline shows maternity-related service closures since January 2022, including:

  • Permanent L&D or NICU closures
  • Temporary closures (noted in the description)
  • Service reductions or consolidations

Each closure entry includes the date, affected service, and a link to the source when available.

How to Cite This Data

For journalists, researchers, and public use:

Suggested Citation:

Ohio Maternity Access Map (2025). [Hospital name or search results]. Retrieved [date] from [URL]

You may download the full dataset as CSV files for analysis. Please attribute the source and verify critical information independently.

Limitations & Disclaimers

  • This information is provided for general reference only and should not be used as a substitute for professional medical advice.
  • Hospital services and capabilities change frequently. Always confirm current services directly with the facility.
  • In an emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room.
  • Travel time estimates are approximate and do not account for real-time traffic or emergency response times.

Updates & Corrections

We strive to keep this data accurate and current. If you notice any errors or have updated information, please help us improve this resource.
